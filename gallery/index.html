Why keep a failed attempt?

The first complete-loop run with ABS/TC crossed 10 m of path error at 6.046 s. This recording shows real and estimated motion during that failure.

  • It is a historical run, not a new restored-Q validation.
  • The later Q adjustment improved a same-measurement estimator replay.
  • Active Q is restored; the saved reduced-Q lap does not establish robustness.
